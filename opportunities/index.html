Opportunities in our lab.

We welcome motivated students and researchers interested in trustworthy, robust, and multimodal AI. Current opportunities and inquiry paths are summarized below.

PhD Opportunities

We currently do not have a fixed PhD opening listed on this page.

Thesis and Guided Research

We are open to working with motivated master’s students on thesis projects and guided research.

Please send your CV, transcript, and a short description of your research interests to tresplab@gmail.com.

Master’s Thesis Opportunities

We are currently looking for motivated Master’s students interested in thesis projects in the following areas:

  • Agent Memory — developing agents that can remember past interactions and continuously learn from experience.
  • Agentic Reinforcement Learning — exploring reinforcement learning methods for training agents, including PPO/GRPO and their variants.
  • Multimodal Reinforcement Learning — improving agents’ ability to understand and reason over multimodal information through reinforcement learning.
  • Agent Harness / Self-Improving Agents — building effective agent systems that can solve complex tasks and improve their capabilities over time.

If you are interested in any of these topics, please contact yan@dbs.ifi.lmu.de with your CV and a short description of your research interests.
