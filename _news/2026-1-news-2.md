Prof. Volker Tresp has contributed a guest article to the latest progress report, "Shaping AI Responsibly," published by the Learning Systems Platform. The report, co-chaired by Federal Minister Dorothee Bär, outlines Germany's roadmap for international competitiveness and technological sovereignty. In his contribution, Prof. Tresp discusses the rapid evolution of Generative AI and the critical balance between innovation and resource efficiency.
