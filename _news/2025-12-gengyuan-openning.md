We are hiring motivated master's students for our research project on Surgical Video Understanding.


**What we offer**:

- Cutting-Edge Research: Work on unsolved problems in multimodal AI and industrial applications of MLLMs.

- Currently enrolled in a relevant Master’s program.

- Strong motivation for open-ended, exploratory research.
- Proficiency in Python and deep learning frameworks (specifically PyTorch).
- Comfortable working in Linux/Shell environments.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills.
