More than 170 audiences visited the online lecture of Ruotong Liao on Monday, 17. March 2025, as an invited speaker at the United Nations "AI for Good". With her talk "Perceive, Remember, and Predict: Explainable Multimodal Agents with Symbolic Representations," Ruotong Liao took part in the online event "Explainable Multimodal Agents with Symbolic Representations & Can AI be less biased?" At the event, which was hosted by the leading platform for artificial intelligence for sustainable development, Ruotong Liao explained her research results, focusing on how the integration of temporal reasoning and symbolic knowledge about evolving events enables LLMs to make structured, interpretable, and context-aware predictions. Ruotong Liao presented work aimed at developing explainable multimodal agents capable of perceiving, remembering, predicting, and justifying their decisions over time.
